Place both players anywhere. Watch the equilibrium update live.

All 6,720 legal positions across the four boards used in positions.pdf — the canonical board, this project's own tackle rule, the territory-reward game, and movement slip — were solved exactly, not approximated by an iterative solver, with NashQIteration.run_exact(). Move either player, hand either one the ball, switch boards, and the equilibrium policy and the full 4×4 Q matrix — as a table or as the same best-response graph the PDF draws — update instantly: no server, no recomputation, just a lookup into the exact solve.
